Grading Policy

This page summarizes how your final grade is determined and the course policies on completing work and deadlines.

1. Grade breakdown

Your final grade is weighted as follows:

Component Weight
Unit quizzes 10%
Coding assignments 15%
Project 1 20%
Project 2 20%
Project 3 35%

2. Completion requirement

You can only pass the class if you complete every assignment. Missing an assignment is not compatible with passing, regardless of your performance on the rest of the work.

3. Deadlines and extensions

If you foresee any reason why you cannot make a deadline, please let us know in advance. It is much easier for us to accommodate you when we have ample heads-up.
